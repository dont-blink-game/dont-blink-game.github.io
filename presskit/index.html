About the game

DON'T BLINK is a survival horror game where the monsters only move when you blink.

Something went terribly wrong deep inside an abandoned research facility. Now you must descend into the darkness and uncover the lost research buried at the very bottom.

Your front camera tracks your eyes in real time. Keep them open and the creatures stay frozen. Blink, and they close in. The deeper you go, the more dangerous they become.

Features

  • Your eyes are the controller. The front camera / webcam tracks your eyes, and every time you blink the monsters close in.
  • An abandoned research facility. Explore dark rooms full of failed experiments, sealed containment tanks and things that should have stayed locked away.
  • Escalating threats. Each step deeper brings new, more dangerous monsters that punish every blink.
  • Monochrome art style. Black, white and grey silhouettes, broken only by flashes of red.
  • Privacy first. Blink detection runs entirely on the device. No face data is stored or sent anywhere.
